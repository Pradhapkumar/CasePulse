## 📖 About the Project

Court judgments are often lengthy PDF documents containing critical legal directives, deadlines, and responsibilities. Manually reviewing these documents is time-consuming and prone to errors.

**CasePulse** leverages Artificial Intelligence, Natural Language Processing (NLP), and OCR technologies to automatically analyze court judgments, extract essential legal information, generate structured action plans, and assist officials in making faster, more informed decisions.

---

## 🚀 Problem Statement

Government officials spend significant time manually reading court judgments to identify:

- Important legal directives
- Responsible departments
- Deadlines
- Compliance requirements
- Actionable tasks

This manual process often results in:

- ⏳ Delayed execution
- ❌ Missed deadlines
- 📄 Human errors
- ⚠️ Inefficient governance

---

## 💡 Our Solution

CasePulse automates the complete workflow by:

✔ Uploading Court Judgment PDFs

✔ AI-based Legal Document Analysis

✔ Automatic Information Extraction

✔ Deadline Detection

✔ Action Plan Generation

✔ Human Verification

✔ Smart Dashboard Visualization

✔ Faster Government Decision-Making
